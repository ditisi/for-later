Briefing

How this works
You are one of two players. This side plans; the page on the right is the other player's, and in a real build they would be on two different screens, talking. The puzzle is only this: walk Wren from the Rail Yard to the Customs Docks without ever standing where a patrol is standing. Green circle = safe when she gets there. Red circle = not. Clicking the circle she is on makes her wait twenty minutes. If you get stuck, press Show me a clean line and watch what it does — then try to beat it.

The point of the whole design is the choice it hides: there is no four-move answer that costs nobody, and a five-move answer that costs nobody exists. Spending a person is always faster. The game lets you, and then tells you what happened to them.
