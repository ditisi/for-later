For Later · level one · Vrbna 7

Same flat. Thirty-one years apart.

In 1995, Jan is having an ordinary Tuesday evening at home. In 2026, a gas main has gone, the stairs have fallen in, and Mira is trapped on the same floor with twelve minutes before the building comes down.

Somehow, they can hear each other. Anything Jan leaves or changes in 1995 is still there for Mira in 2026 — unless thirty-one years got to it first.

How it works. The two halves are the same floor of the same building. Watch 2026 when Jan does something in 1995: a green ring means it survived to 2026, a red one means thirty-one years took it — the radiator got replaced, the cupboard got bricked up, the kitchen went in a skip. Mira can only use what survived. Played by two people, each of you holds one year; the fun is in telling each other what you see. On two screens neither of you gets those rings for the other year — Jan only finds out whether something survived if Mira tells him.
